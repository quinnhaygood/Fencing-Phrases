🤺 Fencing Phrases

Timeline — L and R share each time slot

Each numbered slot is one moment in the phrase. L and R can act in the same slot (simultaneous) or in different slots (before / after). This edition shows the action timeline only — it deliberately does not assign priority or give verdicts. Use it to pose the question to your students; the call is theirs. Annotations you type are for your own scripting only: they are not saved in the JSON and never appear on the exported image. Tick the ⏱ box on consecutive slots to mark them as inside the lockout window — actions there are too close for the box to separate, and the cards carry an "n actions within lockout" badge.
